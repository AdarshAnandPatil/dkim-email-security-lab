# AI ROBOT VIDEO — 16 SCENES

**Style for all scenes:** realistic cinematic 3D, futuristic cybersecurity laboratory, consistent white/silver DKIM robot with cyan eyes, receiver robot, dark red attacker robot, professional educational video.

## 1. Introduction — 15 sec
Narration: “Hello. In this video, we will understand how DomainKeys Identified Mail, or DKIM, protects email communication from tampering and impersonation.”
Prompt: Friendly DKIM robot in futuristic cybersecurity lab, holograms showing email, signature, DNS and verification.

## 2. Email Created — 15 sec
Narration: “First, a sender creates an email. In this example, a college sends an important examination notice to a student.”
Prompt: Person composes email, glowing envelope appears, DKIM robot observes.

## 3. DKIM Bot Receives Email — 15 sec
Narration: “Before the email leaves the sender's domain, the DKIM signing system processes the message.”
Prompt: Glowing email enters secure server room and is received by DKIM robot.

## 4. Canonicalization — 20 sec
Narration: “Before signing, the email undergoes canonicalization. This prepares the message in a consistent format.”
Prompt: Robot puts email into futuristic processor; document becomes standardized.

## 5. Hash Concept — 20 sec
Narration: “Cryptographic processing creates a hash from relevant content. A hash acts like a digital fingerprint.”
Prompt: Email scanned and transformed into glowing fingerprint-like hash visualization.

## 6. Private-Key Signing — 20 sec
Narration: “The sender's domain uses its private key to create a DKIM digital signature.”
Prompt: Secure vault, glowing private key, robot signs floating email.

## 7. Signature Attached — 15 sec
Narration: “The DKIM signature contains information such as the signing domain, selector, algorithm, protected headers, body hash and signature.”
Prompt: Security seal attaches to email with holographic field icons.

## 8. Email Travels — 15 sec
Narration: “After signing, the email travels across the Internet through mail servers.”
Prompt: Signed email travels through global network to receiver.

## 9. Attacker — 20 sec
Narration: “An attacker may attempt to modify the email and deceive the recipient.”
Prompt: Dark red robot intercepts email and alters content; seal becomes unstable.

## 10. Receiver Reads Signature — 15 sec
Narration: “The receiving system reads the signing domain and selector from the DKIM signature.”
Prompt: Receiver robot scans email and identifies domain/selector symbols.

## 11. DNS Lookup — 20 sec
Narration: “The receiving system queries DNS to obtain the public key associated with the signing domain and selector.”
Prompt: Robot sends query to DNS globe/server; public key returns.

## 12. Verification — 20 sec
Narration: “Using the public key, the receiving system verifies the DKIM signature against the signed email content.”
Prompt: Email, signature and public key holograms compared.

## 13. PASS — 15 sec
Narration: “If verification succeeds, DKIM passes. The signed content remained consistent.”
Prompt: Green PASS symbol; email enters secure inbox.

## 14. Tampered Verification — 20 sec
Narration: “For a modified email, verification detects that the received signed content no longer matches.”
Prompt: Two different fingerprint visuals, red mismatch warning.

## 15. FAIL — 15 sec
Narration: “DKIM verification fails because the signed content was modified after signing.”
Prompt: Red FAIL hologram; receiver blocks suspicious email.

## 16. Summary — 20 sec
Narration: “In summary, the sender signs using private key material, and the receiver retrieves the corresponding public key from DNS for verification.”
Prompt: Full animated flow diagram with robots.
