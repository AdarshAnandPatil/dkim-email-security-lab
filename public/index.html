THE REAL-WORLD PROBLEM

Can someone pretend to be your bank or college?

Attackers can spoof addresses and send convincing messages. DKIM adds a domain-level cryptographic signature that receivers can verify.

🕵️

Threat

Attackers attempt to impersonate trusted organizations.

🔐

DKIM

The sender signs selected email content using a private key.

🛡️

Verification

The receiver uses a public key published through DNS.
